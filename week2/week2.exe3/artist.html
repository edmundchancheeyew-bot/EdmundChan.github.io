The Weeknd

The Weeknd, whose real name is Abel Tesfaye, is a Canadian singer, songwriter, and record producer known for his unique blend of R&B, pop, and electronic music. He began his career by anonymously uploading songs to YouTube in 2010, quickly gaining attention for his dark and emotional style. His breakthrough came with the mixtape *House of Balloons*, followed by chart-topping albums like *Beauty Behind the Madness*, *Starboy*, and *After Hours*. The Weeknd is recognized for his haunting vocals, cinematic music videos, and bold artistic vision. Beyond music, he has collaborated with brands like Puma and H&M, and even performed at the Super Bowl halftime show in 2021. His work continues to influence modern pop culture and redefine the boundaries of contemporary music.
